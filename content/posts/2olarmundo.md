# Another Hello, World!

This is my new blog, decided to start it share my knowledge and studies about the FOSS and open knowledge journey, wich started twenty years ago.

The main idea is to write about the possibilities of colaboration, and to start a new journey into open source colaboration, specially by tech writing, that I've trying to study more.

I am also going to share stuff I like, specially on the bridge beetwen arts and software, hardware and internet. Plus some thoughts and learnings from the computer science.

## Computers and internet for a good life

The more I learn about computers, Internet, FOSS, GNU/Linux, Open Hardware, I can make my life is easier and better, with news perpectives for creativity, work and study.

As part of this colaborative universe, it feels like it's not time only to share knowledge, but to build a more precise and transparent road to start new contributions and learning journeys.

Understanding that a big part of our lives is on a digital enviroment, I want to be part of this world, improving the possibilities for my own life, but also improving my own contributions to the FOSS world.
